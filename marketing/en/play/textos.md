RutaWater

## Descripción breve (44/80)

Water delivery route and customer management

## Descripción completa

RutaWater is the ideal tool for water delivery drivers. Organize your daily route, manage your customers and keep track of deliveries, debts and transfers from your phone.

Main features:
• Customer directory with name, address and phone number
• Routes organized by day of the week
• Delivery and order tracking (20L, 12L and 6L bottles and soda)
• Management of debts and pending transfers
• Family links between customers
• Notes and remarks for each customer
• Quick customer search by name or address
• Photos of transfer receipts
• Quick actions: call or message the customer on WhatsApp
• Available in Spanish, English and Portuguese
